## Accuracy locks (do not drift)

1. **AIS fever (ER-AIS-2026-09, patched):** 2026 AIS §4.4: monitor for and treat temperatures **>37.5 °C** to achieve normothermia. No induced hypothermia as routine neuroprotection. ICH INTERACT3 trial-bundle also uses **≤37.5 °C**. Operationally both mean treat at >37.5. **Retire the ≥38.0 AIS cut.** Do not put two different numbers on one board.
2. **Glucose (ER-AIS-2026-GLU):** Treat hypoglycemia **<60 mg/dL**. Persistent hyperglycemia: **140–180 mg/dL** is still reasonable. Intensive control **80–130 is not recommended** (no outcome benefit; more severe hypoglycemia). Treat marked hyperglycemia commonly **>180**.
3. **BP after reperfusion (ER-AIS-2026-BP):** Pre-IVT **<185/110**. Post-IVT 24 h **<180/105**. Intensive SBP **<140 after IVT is not recommended**. After successful EVT, intensive SBP **<140 is harmful and not recommended** (even with TICI 3).
4. **BAO (ER-AIS-2026-BAO):** Strong recommendation for EVT in basilar-artery occlusion within **24 hours** if **NIHSS ≥10** (ATTENTION / BAOCHE). Do **not** apply DAWN/DEFUSE perfusion maps. Effectiveness **not well established** for NIHSS 6–9. GA default. PC-ASPECTS is a local imaging adjunct — do not invent a mandatory numeric cut unless quoting a secondary source as such.
5. **DAWN / DEFUSE 3:** DAWN **6–24 h**; DEFUSE 3 **6–16 h**. Never “6–24 h class.”
6. **Door-to-device ≠ puncture (ER-TS-AT-DEF):** Advanced Therapy door-to-device = arrival to **first pass with the thrombectomy device**. CSTK-09 = arrival to **skin puncture**. Different clocks.
7. **CSTK-03 (ER-CSTK-03T):** Hunt and Hess for SAH; ICH Score for ICH. Prior to surgery **or within 6 hours of arrival** if no surgery. WFNS and modified Fisher are **clinical adjuncts, not the measure**. Submeasures 03a / 03b. Sampling: Yes.
8. **CSTK-04:** Procoagulant reversal for ICH. Confirm live MIF0289 for denominator/agents. Class-specific 2022 ICH reversal (idarucizumab, andexanet/PCC, protamine) is a **clinical SOP**, not automatically the CSTK-04 numerator. CSTK-04 does **not** apply to post-IVT hemorrhagic transformation (that is CSTK-05).
9. **CSTK-06 (ER-CSTK-06T):** First nimodipine **within 24 hours of arrival**. Never give nimodipine IV (U.S. boxed warning). Clinical course commonly 60 mg q4h; hypotension adjustment is local.
10. **TNK / alteplase:** 0.25 mg/kg max 25 **or** alteplase 0.9 mg/kg max 90. TNK **0.4 = Class 3 – No Benefit**. Cardiac card = stop-the-line.
11. **Pregnancy IVT:** Alteplase **may** be used when benefits outweigh risks. TNK pregnancy data **limited**. If house agent is TNK, **stock a labeled alteplase pregnancy path**. Do not withhold imaging or otherwise-indicated reperfusion solely for pregnancy.
12. **Awards ≠ floors:** Honor Roll / Elite / Elite Plus / Advanced Therapy are **published award criteria**, not CSC certification floors. Internal aims may be stricter and must be labeled `internal`.
13. **Operating rhythm:** daily huddle → weekly ops → monthly quality → quarterly stroke executive. No “stroke operations committee” / “stroke governance committee.”
14. **2021 secondary prevention:** When revascularization is indicated, **within 2 weeks** is Class 2a (do not invent 48-hour). LDL-C **<70** statin ± ezetimibe Class 1. Clinic BP **<130/80** Class 1 unless ICH/aSAH/maternal tables apply.
