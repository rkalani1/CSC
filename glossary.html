    Comprehensive Stroke Center Management · 2026.08.2

    Glossary¶

    Operational definitions for Comprehensive Stroke Center management. Definitions are written for Medical Directors, program managers, and quality analysts. Where a Joint Commission or AHA construct is named, the live manual or recognition page remains the official definition.

    Term Definition
    After-action Same-shift structured review of a triggered case (late DTN, DTD outlier, missed reversal or nimodipine, research-clock interference) that captures interval times and a recovery owner.
    aSAH Aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage. CSC volume eligibility uses the live E-App table; the April 2, 2025 Joint Commission announcement reduced the annual criterion to 10.
    ASPECTS Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score. Imaging estimate of early ischemic change used in EVT selection conversations; not a stand-alone treatment rule.
    ASRH Acute Stroke Ready Hospital. Entry-level Joint Commission advanced stroke program focused on initial evaluation, IVT when eligible, and rapid transfer.
    Abstractor Quality analyst who applies STK/CSTK/GWTG specifications to the medical record. The abstractor owns the definition; clinicians own the care.
    Academic measure KPI class that tracks capability-building (trial screens, fellow logs, scholarly output), not just clinical throughput.
    ACGME Program Director Fellowship PD. Distinct from the stroke program manager.
    Accepting physician Named CSC attending who accepts a transfer and starts the receiving-campus clock and capacity plan.
    Achievement measures The seven GWTG-Stroke measures that must each reach 85% for award eligibility (IVT timing, early antithrombotics, VTE, discharge antithrombotics, AF anticoagulation, smoking cessation, intensive statin).
    Advanced Therapy Target: Stroke published award cut: 50% door-to-device ≤90 min for direct arrivals and ≤60 min for transfers (minimum 6 patients). An award, not a CSC certification floor.
    Alteplase IV thrombolytic dosed 0.9 mg/kg, maximum 90 mg, 10% bolus and remainder over 60 minutes, in the 4.5-hour construct (2026 AIS guideline).
    AMD Associate Medical Director. Deputy who can chair weekly ops, take night escalation, and succeed the Medical Director without a coverage hole.
    Balancing measure Metric that detects harm from over-focusing on a process target (mimic overtriage, pharmacy near misses, fellow duty-hour violations, research-clock events).
    BAC Brain Attack Coalition. Issued 2000 PSC recommendations and the 2005 CSC consensus. Foundational history, not a substitute for current TJC standards.
    BAO Basilar-artery occlusion. Posterior-circulation EVT uses a local written rule; do not apply DAWN/DEFUSE cutoffs by habit.
    Bronze / Silver / Gold GWTG-Stroke achievement-award durations: 90 consecutive days, 12 months, and 24 months, each requiring 85% on all seven achievement measures.
    Bypass protocol EMS destination rule that may skip a nearer ASRH/PSC to reach EVT-capable care for suspected LVO. Must be regional, not campus folklore.
    Central IRB Single institutional review board used by multicenter networks (including StrokeNet). Academic CSCs treat familiarity as a research-operations skill.
    CDS / SaMD Clinical decision support / software as a medical device. January 2026 FDA CDS guidance is the current U.S. frame.
    CEA / CAS / TCAR Carotid endarterectomy / stenting / transcarotid artery revascularization.
    Code stroke Hospital-wide activation that assembles ED, imaging, pharmacy, vascular neurology, and IR standby for suspected acute stroke.
    Comfort measures Formal transition to comfort-focused care. A CSTK-01 exclusion when documented day of or day after arrival; must be clinical, not a biased shortcut.
    Coordinator FTE Funded research or program coordinator time. The hidden capacity behind 24/7 screening, abstraction, and 90-day calling.
    CSC Comprehensive Stroke Center. Most demanding Joint Commission advanced stroke program: full hyperacute, hemorrhagic, neurocritical, imaging, peer-review, research, and performance-measurement capability.
    CSTK Comprehensive Stroke measure set. v2026B (operational 3Q–4Q 2026) active IDs: CSTK-01 through CSTK-06 and CSTK-08 through CSTK-12. Same IDs in v2026A (1Q–2Q 2026) and v2027A (1Q–2Q 2027).
    CSTK-01 NIHSS performed for ischemic stroke before recanalization, or within 12 hours if no recanalization, with specified exclusions.
    CSTK-02 Modified Rankin Scale at 90 days. Production clock: enroll at discharge, first attempt by day 60, second by day 75, close in the specification window.
    CSTK-03 Severity measurement for SAH and ICH (overall). Hunt and Hess for SAH (03a); ICH Score for ICH (03b). Prior to surgery or within 6 hours of arrival if no surgery. WFNS and modified Fisher are clinical adjuncts, not the measure. Sampling: Yes.
    CSTK-04 Procoagulant reversal agent initiation for ICH. Confirm live MIF0289 for denominator and agents. Class-specific 2022 ICH reversal (idarucizumab, andexanet/PCC, protamine) is a clinical SOP, not automatically the CSTK-04 numerator. Does not apply to post-IVT hemorrhagic transformation (that is CSTK-05).
    CSTK-05 Hemorrhagic transformation (overall).
    CSTK-06 Nimodipine treatment administered (aSAH). First dose within 24 hours of arrival. Never give nimodipine IV (U.S. boxed warning). Clinical course commonly 60 mg q4h; hypotension adjustment is local.
    CSTK-07 Not in the current (v2026B) set. Do not list, audit, or poster as active.
    CSTK-08 TICI post-treatment reperfusion grade.
    CSTK-09 Arrival time to skin puncture (overall). Not door-to-device (first device pass). Different clocks.
    CSTK-10 mRS at 90 days — favorable-outcome construct.
    CSTK-11 TICI ≥2B within 120 minutes of hospital arrival.
    CSTK-12 TICI ≥2B within 60 minutes of skin puncture.
    CUSP AHRQ Comprehensive Unit-based Safety Program. Unit-level method pairing safety culture with teamwork tools.
    CVT / CVST Cerebral venous (sinus) thrombosis. 2024 AHA statement is the operational source. Not a standard GWTG ischemic / CSTK-01 row.
    CTA / CTP / MRA CT angiography, CT perfusion, and MR angiography. Vascular and tissue imaging used for LVO detection and selected extended-window decisions.
    DAPT Dual antiplatelet therapy. CHANCE/POINT inform use in selected minor non-disabling stroke/TIA; not a substitute for anticoagulation in AF.
    DAWN / DEFUSE 3 Landmark late-window EVT trials. DAWN clock is 6–24 h; DEFUSE 3 clock is 6–16 h. Do not collapse them to a single 6–24 h class.
    DCI Delayed cerebral ischemia after aSAH.
    DIDO Door-in-door-out. Time from arrival at the first hospital to departure for the CSC. A network reliability measure.
    Disabling deficit Clinically important neurologic deficit that qualifies for rapid IVT within 4.5 hours even if the NIHSS number is low (2026 AIS guideline).
    DMAIC Six Sigma sequence: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control. Compatible with PDSA; does not replace a finished small cycle.
    Door-to-CT Interval from campus arrival to CT start. Local operational watch metric; not a published GWTG award threshold in this handbook.
    Door-to-device Arrival to first pass with the thrombectomy device. Not CSTK-09 puncture. See DTD.
    Drip-and-keep Spoke gives IVT and retains the patient when EVT or a higher-level bundle is not indicated.
    Drip-and-drive IVT at the spoke; the endovascular team travels. A design option, not a CSC mandate.
    Drip-and-ship Spoke gives IVT and transfers immediately for EVT or higher-level care.
    DSC Disease-Specific Care. Joint Commission certification framework with three components: standards, clinical practice guidelines, and performance measurement.
    DTD Door-to-device: arrival to first pass with the thrombectomy device. Target: Stroke Advanced Therapy uses ≤90 min direct / ≤60 min transfer. Not CSTK-09 (arrival to skin puncture). Different clocks.
    DTN Door-to-needle. Time from arrival to IV thrombolytic administration. Target: Stroke tiers use 60 / 45 / 30 minute cuts.
    Dual-eligible Patient who meets criteria for both IVT and EVT and should receive both rapidly, without delaying thrombectomy.
    DWI-FLAIR mismatch MRI pattern used to select some unknown-onset patients for extended-window IVT.
    E-App Joint Commission electronic application / eligibility tables. The live source for current CSC volume criteria. Confirm rather than memorize.
    Elite Target: Stroke Honor Roll Elite: 85% of applicable patients with DTN ≤60 minutes. A published award cut, not a CSC certification floor.
    Elite Plus Target: Stroke Honor Roll Elite Plus: 75% DTN ≤45 minutes and 50% DTN ≤30 minutes. A published award cut, not a CSC certification floor.
    Elective carotid intervention CSTK-01 exclusion when the intervention is the reason for admission.
    EMS prenotification Pre-arrival notice from emergency medical services that starts team assembly before the patient crosses the door.
    EMTALA Recipient-hospital obligation: a CSC with specialized capability must accept an appropriate transfer if it has the capacity to treat. No payor screen. See Chapter 19.
    Equity measure KPI slice that tests whether reliability is the same by night/weekend, transfer status, language, sex, race/ethnicity, or payor.
    ESUS Embolic stroke of undetermined source. A workup state, not a final diagnosis.
    EVD External ventricular drain.
    EVT Endovascular therapy (mechanical thrombectomy / MER) for selected ischemic stroke, typically LVO.
    Extended-window IVT IVT beyond 4.5 hours or for unknown onset in selected patients using DWI-FLAIR or perfusion mismatch (2026 AIS guideline).
    Favorable outcome In CSTK-10, the specified favorable 90-day mRS construct. In HERMES reporting, often mRS 0–2 — do not mix the two without labeling.
    Fellowship (vascular neurology) ACGME-accredited training program. A CSC education pillar, not an optional academic extra.
    Fever AIS: treat temperatures >37.5 °C to achieve normothermia (2026 AIS §4.4). ICH INTERACT3 trial-bundle uses ≤37.5 °C. Operationally both mean treat at >37.5. Do not use 38.0 as an AIS cut.
    Floor vs internal target Three classes, not two. Certification floor = live DSC / CSC eligibility or measure specification. Published award cut = Honor Roll / Elite / Elite Plus / Advanced Therapy or GWTG achievement as posted. Internal aim = local management goal, labeled internal. Do not collapse the three.
    FPPE / OPPE Focused and Ongoing Professional Practice Evaluation. Medical-staff processes that must use the same case file the quality committee trusts.
    FTE Full-time equivalent. The worksheet unit for Medical Director, AMD, abstractor, caller, coordinator, and related roles.
    GWTG-Stroke Get With The Guidelines–Stroke. AHA quality program and recognition pathway built on achievement and quality measures.
    Hemorrhagic transformation Post-reperfusion or spontaneous bleed into an infarct; CSTK-05 reports the overall measure.
    HERMES 2016 patient-level meta-analysis of the pivotal EVT trials. mRS 0–2 46.0% vs 26.5%; NNT 2.6 for ≥1-point mRS shift.
    Honor Roll Target: Stroke Honor Roll: 75% DTN ≤60 minutes (minimum 6 patients). A published award cut, not a CSC certification floor.
    HRO High-reliability organizing. Five principles: preoccupation with failure, reluctance to simplify, sensitivity to operations, commitment to resilience, deference to expertise.
    Hub-and-spoke Network model with a CSC hub and ASRH/PSC/TSC spokes connected by EMS, telestroke, and transfer rules.
    Hunt-Hess / WFNS Hunt and Hess is the CSTK-03 instrument for SAH (03a). WFNS and modified Fisher are clinical adjuncts, not the measure. ICH Score is the CSTK-03 instrument for ICH (03b).
    ICH Intracerebral hemorrhage. Operational source: 2022 AHA/ASA ICH guideline; reversal initiation is CSTK-04.
    ICH score Common ICH severity tool used toward CSTK-03.
    IHI Model for Improvement Aim, measures, changes, then PDSA cycles. Default small-cycle method in this handbook.
    In-hospital / in-house stroke Stroke that begins after arrival, including perioperative. Separate denominator from ED-arrival codes; last-known-well still rules. See Chapter 43.
    INTERACT / ENCHANTED2 ICH care-bundle trials that inform bundled blood-pressure and supportive-care operations.
    IVT Intravenous thrombolysis (tenecteplase or alteplase).
    Just culture Accountability model distinguishing human error, at-risk behavior, and reckless behavior.
    Large-core EVT Thrombectomy in selected patients with large ischemic core (SELECT2, ANGEL-ASPECT, RESCUE-Japan LIMIT). Local protocol must define eligibility.
    Last known well (LKW) Time the patient was last observed at neurologic baseline. The clock that all hyperacute decisions reference.
    Leadership designee After-hours owner of the decision-rights matrix.
    Lean Waste-reduction improvement method. Compatible with PDSA when the change is still small enough to finish.
    LVO Large-vessel occlusion. Typical anatomic target for EVT.
    MER Mechanical endovascular reperfusion. Joint Commission language family that includes IA/MER populations in CSTK.
    Mimic Non-stroke diagnosis that entered the code-stroke pathway. A balancing-measure population.
    Mothership EMS transports a suspected LVO candidate directly to an EVT-capable center rather than the nearest hospital.
    mRS Modified Rankin Scale. Functional outcome scored 0–6; required at 90 days for CSTK-02/10.
    mRS 90-day clock Enroll at discharge, first attempt by day 60, second by day 75, close in the specification window.
    MSU Mobile stroke unit. Ambulance with imaging and treatment capability; endorsed in the 2026 AIS guideline.
    NCC Neurocritical care. Dedicated neuro ICU capability is a historical and operational CSC expectation.
    NIHSS National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale. Required for CSTK-01 as specified. Does not, by itself, exclude a disabling deficit from 4.5-hour IVT.
    Nimodipine Standard aSAH medication; CSTK-06 clocks first dose within 24 hours of arrival. Never give nimodipine IV (U.S. boxed warning). Clinical course commonly 60 mg q4h; hypotension adjustment is local. 2023 aSAH guideline is the operational source.
    NIR Neurointerventional radiology / endovascular neurosurgery service that performs EVT and selected aneurysm procedures.
    NNT Number needed to treat. HERMES reported NNT 2.6 for ≥1-point mRS shift with EVT vs control.
    On-call escalation card Pocket-level page tree stating who is called, in what order, for code stroke, IR, reversal, transfer pressure, and stop-the-line.
    Operating rhythm Daily huddle → weekly ops → monthly quality → quarterly stroke executive. After-action is a triggered ritual, not a fifth standing forum.
    Operating-system card One-page map of pillar owners, standing meetings, defect-routing rules, and stop-the-line authority.
    Outcome measure KPI that tracks what happened to the patient (90-day mRS, symptomatic HT, mortality — the last labeled if unadjusted).
    PDSA Plan-Do-Study-Act. One-page improvement cycle with a numeric prediction and a 90-day kill/close date.
    Pediatric AIS Childhood acute ischemic stroke. First dedicated recommendations appear in the 2026 AIS guideline.
    Peer review Privileged case review for quality and professionalism. Must have a path that the night team can find.
    Performance measurement The DSC component that includes STK/CSTK (and related) reporting. Not optional for CSC.
    Perfusion mismatch Imaging pattern used to select some 4.5–9 hour or unknown-onset patients for IVT or late-window EVT.
    PFO Patent foramen ovale. Closure evaluation is a clinic/cardiology pathway, not a day-2 implant.
    Plus award GWTG recognition that adds quality-measure compliance on top of the seven achievement measures.
    Process measure KPI that tracks whether the right action happened on time (DTN, CSTK-09, STK-5).
    Procoagulant reversal Timed initiation of agents that reverse anticoagulation or coagulopathy in ICH (CSTK-04).
    PSC Primary Stroke Center. Joint Commission advanced program with IVT and organized inpatient stroke care; transfers complex cases.
    RACI Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed. Decision-rights grid used so pillars do not collide.
    Recanalization / reperfusion Opening of an occluded vessel / restoration of flow. TICI grades reperfusion after EVT.
    Research-clock event Any trial screen, consent, or coordinator action that adds documented minutes to needle or puncture. Internal target is zero.
    SCS26 2026 Stroke Certification Standards book. Confirm live when quoting standards language.
    Site PI Site principal investigator. The physician owner of a trial at the CSC; a StrokeNet capability requirement.
    Skin puncture Time of arterial puncture for EVT. Start of the CSTK-12 interval.
    SOP Standard operating procedure. Handbook skeletons are samples until locally approved.
    Spoke Referring hospital in the hub-and-spoke network (ASRH, PSC, TSC, or undesignated).
    STK Inpatient stroke core measure set: STK-1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10.
    STK-OP Outpatient/ED stroke measure family in v2026B. Confirm current specifications.
    STK-3 Anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation/flutter at discharge (also GWTG achievement measure 5).
    STK-4 Inpatient thrombolytic-therapy measure. Do not confuse with Target: Stroke DTN.
    STK-6 Discharged on statin per the STK specification. Not the same as the GWTG achievement intensive-statin measure.
    Stop-the-line Authority of any clinician to halt an unsafe step (wrong dose card, stock-out, research interference) and escalate the same shift.
    StrokeNet NIH national network for multicenter stroke trials in acute care, prevention, and recovery.
    StrokeNet National Coordinating Center StrokeNet’s national clinical coordinating hub. Do not abbreviate as NCC in CSC documents; NCC means neurocritical care.
    Stroke program manager RN/operational dyad partner. Distinct from the ACGME Program Director.
    Structural measure KPI that asks whether the system can still run Tuesday night (call coverage, stock, abstractor backlog, rolling volumes).
    Survey tracer Surveyor method that follows one patient through people, documents, and time stamps rather than through an org chart.
    SWOT Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. Offsite tool that must translate into bets or a parked list.
    Target: Stroke AHA time-based recognition program (Honor Roll, Elite, Elite Plus, Advanced Therapy). Phase III national goals match Elite / Advanced Therapy primary goals. Each tier is a published award cut, not a CSC certification floor.
    Target: Type 2 Diabetes GWTG composite recognition requiring ≥80% for 12 months.
    Teach-back Patient demonstrates understanding of STK-8 content.
    Telestroke Remote vascular-neurology evaluation that issues a time-critical recommendation and, when indicated, a transfer decision.
    Tenecteplase (TNK) IV thrombolytic dosed 0.25 mg/kg IV push, maximum 25 mg (2026 AIS guideline; EXTEND-IA TNK). 0.4 mg/kg is not recommended (Class 3 – No Benefit). Do not use a coronary-dose card.
    TICI Thrombolysis in Cerebral Infarction scale. CSTK-08 documents the grade; CSTK-11/12 use TICI ≥2B as effective reperfusion.
    TIA Transient ischemic attack. Disposition follows the 2021 secondary-prevention guideline; do not invent an ABCD2 cutoff here.
    Tracer script Pre-written walkthrough of IVT, transfer EVT, ICH reversal, aSAH nimodipine, or 90-day mRS used in mock and real surveys.
    Transfer center 24/7 intake function that runs the transfer checklist, names the accepting physician, and never leaves a sender without a next step.
    TSC Thrombectomy-Capable Stroke Center. Joint Commission advanced program with 24/7 EVT but not the full CSC portfolio.
    Unknown-onset / wake-up stroke Stroke with unclear LKW. May qualify for extended-window IVT or late-window EVT under imaging-based local protocols.
    Vascular neurology Physician specialty that owns the medical stroke service, code-stroke decision, and typically the Medical Director role.
    VTE prophylaxis Venous thromboembolism prevention. STK-1 and GWTG achievement measure 3.
